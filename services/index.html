Pre-Purchase Inspection

$59.99

Trip fee and mileage may apply for locations more than a 30-minute drive away.

Buying a used car? We meet you where the car is, at the seller’s driveway or the lot, and check it over before you commit, so you know what you’re buying.

What we check

  • Computer scan for stored and pending trouble codes, plus readiness monitors (they show whether codes were cleared recently)
  • Engine and transmission: leaks, fluid level and condition, belts and hoses
  • Brakes, tires (tread and age), suspension and steering
  • Battery and charging system
  • Rust underneath: frame, subframe and brake lines (New Jersey road salt makes this worth a look)
  • Lights, wipers, and signs of past body or flood repair
  • A short test drive, when the seller allows it

What you get: a plain-language summary of what we found, what needs attention soon, and what can wait, so you can decide, or ask the seller about price.

Ask the seller’s permission first; dealerships usually allow an outside inspection on their lot.

Why choose us

  • We come to the car; no need to bring it to a shop
  • An independent second opinion before you sign
  • Ask us anything on the spot

Privacy Upgrades

$159.99 per hour, one-hour minimum, plus parts

Many newer cars send driving data, such as location, speed, and braking, back to the manufacturer. If you’d rather keep that data private, we can disable it.

We offer

  • DCM (Data Communication Module) removal or bypass: disables the module that reports your vehicle’s data.
  • Cellular antenna bypass: disconnects the vehicle’s cellular connection so data can’t be transmitted even if the module is left in place.

Why choose us

  • Done correctly, without affecting unrelated systems (infotainment, Bluetooth, and speakers often share wiring with these modules)
  • Clear explanation of what’s being disabled and why
