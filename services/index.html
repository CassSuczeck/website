Privacy Upgrades

$159.99 per hour, one-hour minimum, plus parts

Many newer cars send driving data, such as location, speed, and braking, back to the manufacturer. If you’d rather keep that data private, we can disable it.

We offer

  • DCM (Data Communication Module) removal or bypass: disables the module that reports your vehicle’s data.
  • Cellular antenna bypass: disconnects the vehicle’s cellular connection so data can’t be transmitted even if the module is left in place.

Why choose us

  • Done correctly, without affecting unrelated systems (infotainment, Bluetooth, and speakers often share wiring with these modules)
  • Clear explanation of what’s being disabled and why
