Our warranty

Our labor is covered for 90 days or 3,000 miles, whichever comes first.

What it covers

  • The labor listed on your signed written estimate.
  • Parts carry their manufacturer’s warranty, and we pass that warranty on to you.

How we make it right

If work we did isn’t right within the warranty period, we’ll either redo the repair or replace the parts, or refund what you paid for that work. We choose which.

How to make a claim

Call or text (908) 986-3415 or email info@besafetravels.com. We’ll set a time and place to look at the vehicle and any faulty parts; any cost of getting the vehicle there is yours.

What it doesn’t cover

  • Parts you supplied
  • Neglect, misuse, abuse, modification, alteration or unreasonable use
  • Damage from improper maintenance
  • Racing or off-road use
  • Problems the vehicle already had

Your rights under the law

Nothing in our warranty takes away rights you have under the Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act or New Jersey law.
