Privacy policy

Be Safe Travels LLC, doing business as Safe Travels Mobile Repair (“we”, “us”), runs this website and our tools at tools.besafetravels.com. We collect only what we need to answer you and fix your car. We never sell your information.

What we collect

What you give us

  • Forms on this site: the contact form asks for your name, phone number, email, your vehicle, your town, and what’s going on with it. The forms on our Terms and Conditions page ask for your first and last name and record that you agreed.
  • Repair estimate calculator and “Fix it, or trade it in?” tool (tools.besafetravels.com): if you ask us to contact you, your name, phone, email, your vehicle and the estimate. If you enter a VIN, your browser sends it to the U.S. government’s free NHTSA vehicle lookup to find the year, make and model.
  • Booking: what you enter when you schedule a call through Calendly.
  • Calls and texts: your phone number and what you tell us.

What’s collected automatically

  • Hosting: GitHub, which hosts this website and our tools, logs visits like any web host (for example your IP address, browser, and the pages requested).
  • Fonts: this site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when the fonts load.
  • Booking page: our booking page shows Calendly’s scheduler, which may set its own cookies.
  • Spam protection: our forms use a hidden field and a timing check to tell people from bots. They don’t track you.

This site itself doesn’t set cookies or keep visitor statistics. We don’t take payments on this website, and we don’t ask for Social Security, driver’s license or card numbers here.

How we use it

To answer your request, give you a price, schedule and do the work, follow up about that work, honor our warranty, and keep the site working and free of spam. We don’t send marketing texts.

Who else handles it

We don’t sell your information or share it for advertising. A few services help us run the business and handle it for us, not for their own marketing:

  • GitHub: hosts this website and tools.besafetravels.com.
  • Google: our business email, the spreadsheet where form and tool requests arrive, Google Fonts, and Google Voice (our business phone line, for calls and texts).
  • Calendly: booking.
  • The software we use for estimates and invoices.
  • Carfax: after a repair, we report the vehicle’s service record (VIN, mileage and the work done) to Carfax’s vehicle history, without your name or contact details.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights or someone’s safety.

Calls and texts

When you text us, or give us your number on a form and agree to be contacted, we may call or text you about your request: replies, estimates, scheduling, arrival times, and follow-up on the work. How often depends on the job. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop texts, or HELP for help. Agreeing to texts is not a condition of buying anything from us.

We never sell or share your mobile number or your text consent with anyone for their marketing.

Cookies: your choices

This site doesn’t set cookies itself. Calendly’s scheduler on our booking page may; you can block or clear cookies in your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may stop the scheduler from working.

How long we keep it

As long as we need it to do the work, stand behind it, and keep the business, tax and repair records the law requires. Then we delete it.

Keeping it safe

We limit who can see your information and use services with secure (HTTPS) connections. No website is perfectly secure, so please don’t send sensitive details like card numbers by form, email or text.

Children

This site is for adults. We don’t knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Your choices

To see, correct, or delete what we have about you, call or text (908) 986-3415 or email info@besafetravels.com. We’ll answer within 30 days. We may need to keep some records the law requires.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll post the new version here with a new date.
