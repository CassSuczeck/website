Privacy policy

We collect only what we need to answer you and fix your car. We never sell your information.

What we collect

  • Contact form and estimate calculator: your name, phone, email (optional), vehicle, town, and what’s going on with the car.
  • Booking: what you enter when you schedule a call through Calendly.
  • Calls and texts: your number and what you tell us.

We don’t collect Social Security numbers, driver’s license numbers, or card numbers on this website.

How we use it

To answer your request, give you a price, schedule and do the work, and follow up about that work. We don’t send marketing texts.

Who else handles it

A few services help us run the site: Calendly (booking), Google (fonts on this site and our email and form tools), and Google Voice (our business phone line, for calls and texts). They process your information for us, not for their own marketing. We never sell or share your mobile number or text consent for anyone’s marketing.

Calls and texts

When you text us or give us your number on a form, we may call or text you about your request: replies, estimates, scheduling, arrival times, and follow-up on the work. How often depends on the job. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop texts, or HELP for help.

Your choices

To see, correct, or delete what we have about you, call or text (908) 986-3415 or email info@besafetravels.com. Reply STOP to any text to stop texts.
