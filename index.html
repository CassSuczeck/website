Car care that’s transparent, professional, and convenient

Whether you’re juggling school drop-off, a packed work schedule, or getting ready for a road trip, we bring maintenance, diagnostics, repairs, and accessory installs to your driveway. No surprise charges: you get a written estimate before any work begins. On business days, we return every voicemail the same day and can often schedule a diagnostic visit the same day too.

What we fix

  • Brakes: pads, rotors, and full brake jobs
  • Batteries, alternators, and charging problems
  • Check engine lights and no-starts
  • Belts, pulleys, and suspension
  • Small fixes: bulbs, sun visors, mirrors, door latches
  • Dash cams, tow hitches, and other accessories
  • Pre-purchase and multipoint inspections

How pricing works

  • Free phone consultation: tell us what’s going on, and we’ll give you our best read and a ballpark price.
  • Repairs and on-site diagnostics: $159.99 per hour, one-hour minimum. Diagnostic time is credited toward the repair if you go ahead.
  • Small jobs (a bulb, a mirror): $25 trip fee plus parts and mileage, or bundle a few to reach the one-hour minimum and we waive the trip fee and mileage.
