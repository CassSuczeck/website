Who we are

Safe Travels Mobile Repair is a family-owned mobile auto repair service based in Middletown, NJ, that brings expert and honest auto care to you, on your schedule, so your car and life stay on track.

We proudly serve drivers within about 30 minutes of Middletown, including Red Bank, Holmdel, and Hazlet.

Founded by Feather Waters, a seasoned technician with 16 years of experience, we specialize in on-site car diagnostics, vehicle inspections, and mobile car repairs that fit your schedule and lifestyle. Feather’s expertise spans from passenger cars to heavy-duty equipment, like semis and excavators. Whether you need a pre-purchase car inspection, help with a check engine light, or a solution for slow-moving windows or electrical issues, she’ll get to the root of the problem and explain it clearly before any work begins.

Because Feather knows how disruptive car trouble can be, Safe Travels returns every voicemail the same day on business days and, in many cases, can schedule a diagnostic visit the same day.
