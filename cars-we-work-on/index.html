Cars we work on

We work on most gas and hybrid cars, SUVs, trucks and vans from model year 1996 on, domestic and imported.

American

Buick, Cadillac, Chevrolet, Chrysler, Dodge, Ford, GMC, Hummer, Jeep, Lincoln, Mercury, Oldsmobile, Plymouth, Pontiac, Ram, Saturn

Japanese and Korean

Acura, Genesis, Honda, Hyundai, Infiniti, Isuzu, Kia, Lexus, Mazda, Mitsubishi, Nissan, Scion, Subaru, Suzuki, Toyota

European

Alfa Romeo, Audi, BMW, Fiat, Jaguar, Land Rover, Maserati, Mercedes-Benz, Mini, Porsche, Saab, Smart, Volkswagen, Volvo

For imported makes, work varies a lot from model to model, so we confirm the price after a look.

Where we refer you to a specialist

  • Electric vehicles: we do tow hitch and accessory installs, and in some cases brakes, suspension and interior work. For anything else, we’ll point you to a shop that does.
  • Cars older than model year 1996, before standard OBD-II diagnostics.
  • Exotics such as Ferrari, Lamborghini, Bentley, Rolls-Royce, Aston Martin, McLaren and Lotus, and Maserati’s MC20 and MCPura.
